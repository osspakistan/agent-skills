# Prompt: Opinion / Commentary

Use for: op-ed articles, opinion columns, personal essays with a position, commentary pieces, hot take videos, reaction videos, creator argument videos, "my thoughts on X" videos.

---

You're summarizing this for someone who wants to understand the creator's or author's take and decide if it holds up.

Focus: What's their argument? What triggered it? Is it well-supported?

---

## Output Format

TITLE: [Author / Creator: Honest Headline or Hot Take]

**Opening (no header)**
One sentence: What is the main argument, thesis, or hot take?

## [*keep this section head dynamic. Keep it conversational and max 3 words. Use casual language. Examples: "What Happened", "The Spark", "Context". Unlike standard headings, do NOT use the `The {xyz}` pattern*]

What prompted this? (News event, controversy, personal experience, market trend):
- 1-2 sentences giving the trigger and background.

## [*keep this section head dynamic. Keep it conversational and max 3 words. Use casual language. Examples: "Their Case", "Evidence", "Why They Think". Unlike standard headings, do NOT use the `The {xyz}` pattern*]

How do they support their argument? (3-5 bullets total):
- Supporting point one
- Supporting point two
- Evidence, benchmark, or counter-argument addressed

**Reality Check**
Based on the evidence presented: Is this take well-supported, partially supported, or mostly vibes? One sharp, unhedged sentence.

## [*keep this section head dynamic. 2-3 words matching the vibe. Examples: "Best Line", "Sharp Quote", "Mic Drop". Do NOT use the `The {xyz}` pattern*]

One memorable, punchy line from the piece:
> [Quote]

*(Timestamp if video)*

---

### Strict Heading & Quality Rules:
- Dynamic headers MUST be conversational, max 3 words, and NEVER use `The {xyz}`.
- Tone: Fair but critical. Help someone evaluate the argument, not just accept it.
