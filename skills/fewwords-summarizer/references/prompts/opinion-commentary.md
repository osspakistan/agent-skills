# Prompt: Opinion / Commentary

Use for: op-ed articles, opinion columns, personal essays with a position, commentary pieces, hot take videos, reaction videos, creator argument videos, "my thoughts on X" videos.

---

You're summarizing this for someone who wants to understand the creator's or author's take and decide if it holds up.

Focus: What's their argument? What triggered it? Is it well-supported?

---

## Output Format

TITLE: [Author / Creator: Honest Headline or Hot Take]

**Opening (no header)**
State the speaker's main argument in one sentence.

**Body (no fixed headings)**
Explain what prompted the argument, then walk through the strongest supporting points in the order a reader needs them. Prefer connected paragraphs. Use bullets only when the source has distinct points that are easier to scan; do not target a fixed number or repeat one claim in several sections. Unless the user asks for detail, aim for 100–160 words and do not exceed 180; select up to three essential supporting details instead of retelling every section.

Distinguish evidence from opinion where that difference matters. Keep uncertainty when the evidence is incomplete; do not force a firm verdict. For video sources, attribute opinions to the named presenter or host, never to “this video” or “the author of the video”; state straightforward facts directly. Include a memorable quote only when it adds something the summary does not already explain; do not add a stock heading for it.

*(Timestamp if video and a quote is included)*

---

### Strict Heading & Quality Rules:
- Body headings are optional. Use one only when it makes the summary easier to follow; keep it specific and short (usually 1–3 words). Never start a body heading with “The” or use “Takeaway(s)” / “Conclusion” as a stock label.
- Tone: Fair but critical. Help someone evaluate the argument, not just accept it.
