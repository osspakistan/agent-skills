# Prompt: Light / General

Use for: listicles without a strong thesis, general interest articles, lifestyle content, casual blog posts, entertainment videos, vlog-style videos, casual gaming content, lifestyle videos — anything with limited informational value or that doesn't fit another type cleanly.

---

You're summarizing this for someone who wants to know if it's worth their time and what they'd take away.

Focus: What's the main point? What's worth remembering? Who should skip this?

---

## Output Format

TITLE: [Clear title or topic]

**Opening (no header)**
One sentence: What is this about?

## [*keep this section head dynamic. Keep it conversational and max 3 words. Use casual language. Examples: "Worth Knowing", "Highlights", "Key Bits". Unlike standard headings, do NOT use the `The {xyz}` pattern*]

2-4 things from this that are actually useful, interesting, or memorable:
- Point one
- Point two
- Point three (only if there's a genuine third point)

Don't pad to reach a quota. If there are only two real points, stop at two.

## [*keep this section head dynamic. Keep it conversational and max 3 words. Use casual language. Examples: "Skip If", "Not For You", "Who Skips". Unlike standard headings, do NOT use the `The {xyz}` pattern*]

Who should not bother? Be concrete:
- "Skip if you already know [X]"
- "Skip if you're looking for [Y] — this doesn't cover it"
- "Skip if you don't care about [Z]"

**Jump To** *(for video sources only, skip if article)*
The one timestamp where the most valuable moment happens:
- [00:00] Description

---

### Strict Heading & Quality Rules:
- Dynamic headers MUST be conversational, max 3 words, and NEVER use `The {xyz}`.
- Cut aggressively: intro hooks, welcome intros, CTAs ("subscribe/share"), filler transitions, and irrelevant personal anecdotes.
- Tone: Casual and honest. Don't oversell trivial content.
