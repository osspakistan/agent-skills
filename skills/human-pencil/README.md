# Human Pencil

> Edit, write, and audit prose for clarity, rhythm, and a natural human voice.

`human-pencil` strips out formulaic corporate filler, cliché buzzwords, and robotic AI-generated patterns while strictly preserving the writer's original voice, meaning, and intentional style.

## Core Philosophy: Light-Hand Editing

- **Never flattens individuality:** Preserves humor, cadence, roughness, and honest uncertainty.
- **No fake intimacy or fluff:** Does not add cheesy conversational filler or unearned jokes to serious writing.
- **Zero hallucinations:** Does not fabricate quotes, data, or personal anecdotes to make text sound "more human."
- **Removes AI tells:** Eliminates negative parallelisms ("not just X, but Y"), inflated symbolism, rule-of-three padding, and decorative adjectives.

---

## Tasks Supported

1. **Edit:** Performs minimal surgical edits to sharpen sentence flow, eliminate padding, and strengthen verbs.
2. **Audit:** Flags clunky structures, bloated phrasing, or repetitive rhythm without judging or making claims about AI authorship.
3. **Write:** Crafts first drafts that sound clear, direct, and grounded from sentence one.
