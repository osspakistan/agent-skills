# Musk Email Writer

> Craft internal memos and decisive business communications in the direct, numbers-driven style of Elon Musk.

`musk-email-writer` is a style pastiche skill grounded in a corpus of ~200 real business emails spanning Tesla, SpaceX, X/Twitter, and Neuralink. It helps founders, leaders, and operators cut through corporate fluff and communicate with urgency, first-principles logic, and complete clarity.

> [!WARNING]
> **Style Pastiche Disclaimer:** This skill produces original emails in a recognizable voice. Generated outputs are for your own business communications and must **never** be presented or represented as genuine emails sent by Elon Musk.

## Core Voice Principles

1. **Lead with the verdict:** The first sentence states the ask or the hard truth immediately. No warm-up filler.
2. **Quantified urgency:** Replaces vague adjectives with numbers, specific dates, and named responsible owners.
3. **First-principles reasoning:** Explains *why* using the actual physical, economic, or logistical constraints in one concise paragraph.
4. **Extreme economy:** Emails are as short as possible. Unnecessary words and corporate pleasantries are discarded.

## Covered Scenarios & Patterns

- **All-Hands Rallies & Sprints:** Quarter-end pushes, critical deadlines, manufacturing goals.
- **Cost Discipline & Headcount:** Spending freezes, reorgs, resource consolidation.
- **Directives & Company Standards:** Meetings policy, communication chains, return-to-office expectations.
- **Terse Approvals & Declines:** Fast one-liners ("Consider it done", "No, for the following reason").
