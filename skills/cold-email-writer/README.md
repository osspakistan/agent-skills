# Cold Email Writer

> Write, rewrite, and critique cold outreach emails that busy strangers actually answer.

`cold-email-writer` helps founders, sales reps, and operators craft concise, high-converting cold emails and DMs. Grounded in frameworks from Y Combinator, OpenVC, Alex Hormozi's offer structures, and terse executive communication styles, it cuts out fake flattery and generic hooks, focusing instead on **clarity, checkable proof, and low-friction asks**.

## Core Philosophy: Proof-First & Zero Fluff

1. **Under 60 seconds to read:** 3 to 5 sentences max (under ~100 words).
2. **No fake personalization:** Kills the generic "saw you on a podcast" or "congrats on funding" filler that immediately flags an email as an automated template.
3. **Checkable proof upfront:** Leads with concrete metrics, customer names, pilot results, or verifiable traction.
4. **Low-friction asks:** Asks for something small (permission to send a short video, an intro, quick advice) rather than demanding 30 minutes of a stranger's calendar.

---

## Covered Outreach Scenarios

- **Investor & Fundraising Outreach:** Pre-seed to Series A founder pitches with checkable traction facts.
- **B2B Sales & Customer Prospecting:** Outcome-first offers identifying specific, quantifiable pain points.
- **Partnerships & Business Development:** Concrete mutual value propositions without corporate jargon.
- **Recruiting & Sourcing:** Direct notes to high-demand engineering or executive candidates.
- **Follow-ups to Ignored Emails:** High-signal, polite nudges that add new proof or context instead of annoying "bumping this" messages.
