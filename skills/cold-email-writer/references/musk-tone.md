# Musk-style tone (optional pass)

Style only. Never present output as a real Musk email.

## Moves that transfer to cold email
- **No warm-up.** First line is the ask or the situation ("Any interest in participating in the Twitter deal?").
- **Short.** The strongest examples are 1-3 lines.
- **Numbers and deadlines.** Specific figures, named dates, named owners.
- **Reason, then directive.** One line of why, then what to do. ("Very high potential and I'd rather have you than anyone else.")
- **Name the number.** Don't wait for the other side to anchor (e.g. he proposed the investment size).
- **Scarcity as fact, not tactic.** "The round is oversubscribed" only if true.
- **Plain, slightly blunt.** Occasional wry line is fine; hype words are not.

## Limits
- The corpus is mostly internal or warm. Brevity worked because credibility and context already existed (e.g. the Ellison text exchange was with a known contact).
- A cold sender can copy the shape (no hook, specific number, short reason) but not the shortcuts. Proof must replace earned credibility.
- Do not add rudeness, insults, or ultimatums; those appear in the corpus but hurt cold outreach.
- For Tier-4 situations (cold sales, recruiting), say the voice is extrapolated.
