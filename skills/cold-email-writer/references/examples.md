## 1. Investor outreach

### Before (reads as template)
> Hi John,
> Heard you on the OpenVC podcast last week. Congrats on that Otoro seed! Since you're big on vertical SaaS, you'll want to hear about what we're building here at Nickelback.
> Financial institutions spend $18B per year manually doing QA, a process we have fully automated.
> A few facts: CEO 8 yrs enterprise sales (Adobe, Microsoft); CTO ex-lead dev at Experian; MVP delivered proven 10x ROI; 2 paid pilots; $500k pipeline.
> We're raising $750k pre-seed to get to $1M ARR. 30% committed. Open to a 30-min call next week?
> Cheers, Chad

Problems: flattery + category-tag hook; unsourced $18B; "proven 10x ROI" from an MVP; never says what the product does; best proof (paid pilots) buried; heavy ask; recognizable formula.

### After
> Subject: Automated bank QA, 2 paid pilots
>
> John,
>
> We automate QA testing for financial institutions. Banks do it manually today, which is slow and expensive.
>
> - 2 paid pilots live; early users report ~10x ROI [state how measured]
> - $500k ARR pipeline
> - CEO: 8 yrs enterprise sales (Adobe, Microsoft). CTO: ex-lead dev at Experian.
>
> Raising $750k pre-seed to reach $1M ARR, 30% committed.
>
> If this fits, I'll send a 2-min demo video.
>
> Chad

## 2. Sales outreach (non-investor)

### Before
> Hope this email finds you well! I came across your LinkedIn and was so impressed by your journey at Acme. I'd love to pick your brain about how we can help you leverage AI to supercharge your support team. Do you have 30 minutes this week?

### After
> Subject: Cutting Acme's ticket backlog
>
> Hi Maria,
>
> We resolve ~40% of Tier-1 support tickets automatically for [Customer A] and [Customer B] ([metric, timeframe]).
>
> You're hiring 6 support agents on your careers page, so I figure backlog is a live issue. Want me to send a 1-page summary of how it works?
>
> Sam, founder at [Company]

Why: says what it does, proof is named and checkable, the personalization is a real observable tied to the offer (passes the swap test), the ask is small.

## 3. Terse tone (blunt variant)
> Subject: Intro to [Name]?
>
> [Name],
>
> We make [X]. [Number] paying customers, [growth]. Raising [amount] to [specific use].
>
> Could you intro me to [Person]? Two lines I can forward.
>
> [Sender]

## Bad vs good checklist
| Bad | Good |
|---|---|
| "Hope this finds you well" | First line states what you do |
| "Congrats on your recent raise!" | Cut, or a specific substantive reference |
| "Would love 30 min to chat" | "If this fits, I'll send a 2-min demo" |
| "Revolutionary AI platform" | "We automate QA testing for banks" |
| "Proven 10x ROI" | "~10x ROI in 2 pilots, measured as [how]" |
