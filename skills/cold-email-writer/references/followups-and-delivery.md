# Follow-ups and delivery

## Follow-up cadence
Follow up 2-4 times, a few days apart. Each follow-up must add something, not just "bumping this".

- **Touch 2 (day 3-4)**: one new proof point or fact, plus the same small ask.
- **Touch 3 (day 7-8)**: one line. Easy out: "Not a fit? Tell me and I'll stop."
- **Touch 4 (optional, ~day 14)**: a useful asset (demo link, short metric) or a better-targeted ask (intro to the right person).
- Keep each under 40 words. Reply in the same thread.

Example touch 2:
> John, quick add: a third bank signed a paid pilot this week. Happy to send the 2-min demo if useful.

## If ignored
Don't get mad or send guilt-trip emails. Check back in a few months with new news (a milestone, a new customer).

## Deliverability pre-send check
A perfect email dies if the sending domain is misconfigured. Before a campaign, confirm:
- SPF, DKIM, DMARC pass on the sending domain (re-check periodically; they can silently break).
- Sent from a company email with the sender's name in it.
- Founders: send by hand at first, no automation.
- Targeted list: 100 good-fit beat 1,000 random.
