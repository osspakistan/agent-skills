# Human Pencil DET

> Natural English prose with a rich, contextual vocabulary bank for Duolingo English Test (DET) practice.

`human-pencil-det` merges the light-touch human editing of `human-pencil` with a curated DET vocabulary bank. It helps learners and test-takers express sophisticated ideas naturally without falling into the trap of forced or robotic vocabulary stuffing.

---

## Quick Install

Install just this skill using [`skills.sh`](https://skills.sh):

```bash
# Project-level
npx skills add osspakistan/agent-skills --skill human-pencil-det

# Or globally across all your coding agents
npx skills add osspakistan/agent-skills --skill human-pencil-det -g
```

---

## How It Works

1. **Context-First Word Choice:** Recommends and weaves in advanced vocabulary where it fits the context naturally, providing clarity rather than pretense.
2. **DET Test Readiness:** Tailors practice passages, writing prompts, and feedback to the expectations of the Duolingo English Test.
3. **Avoids Robotic Stifling:** Ensures test responses flow logically, vary in sentence structure, and maintain authentic human expression.

---

## Tasks Supported

- **DET Writing Practice:** Generates test-style prompts and demonstrates high-scoring sample responses using natural vocabulary.
- **Essay Edit & Polish:** Upgrades simple words to precise alternatives without making the essay sound awkward or artificial.
- **Pattern Audit:** Identifies overused words and suggests precise synonyms from the DET vocabulary bank.
